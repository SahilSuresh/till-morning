Till Morning is an iPhone app that puts a short pause between you and the shopping and takeaway apps
you choose. It is made by [your name or company], [UK address] ("we").

## The short version

- No account and no login.
- No bank links. We never see your payments, baskets, prices or receipts.
- Your apps, budgets, goals, check-ins, XP and ranks stay on your iPhone.
- We collect a small amount of anonymous technical data to keep the app working and to fix crashes.
- We don't sell data, show ads or track you across other apps and websites.

## What stays on your iPhone

The apps you pick in Apple's Screen Time picker, your shields and Shopping Passes, budgets, goals,
your own shield message, check-in answers, XP, ranks, Morning card stamps and bank-limit seals.
Apple gives Till Morning only private tokens for the apps you pick, not their names, and nothing about
what you do inside them. We can't see any of this, and it is never sent to us.

## What we collect, and why

| What | Why | Who processes it |
|---|---|---|
| Crash reports (what went wrong in the app, device model, iOS and app version, a random install ID). No IP address. | To find and fix crashes | Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.) |
| App performance and a short list of anonymous events, such as "setup finished" or "Shopping Pass started", with a random install ID. Never your apps, budgets or anything you type. | To see whether the app is fast and whether setup and the free trial work | Expo (650 Industries, Inc.) |
| Subscription status, App Store receipt and an anonymous customer ID | To run your free trial and subscription and restore purchases | RevenueCat, Inc. and Apple |

Apple handles your payment. We never see your card or Apple Account details.

Our legal basis under UK GDPR is legitimate interests (keeping the app working and improving it) and,
for subscriptions, performing our contract with you.

## How long we keep it

Crash reports are deleted after [90] days. Anonymous usage data is kept for up to [12] months.
Subscription records are kept while you subscribe and for as long as needed for tax and accounting.

## Deleting your data

- **On your iPhone:** Settings → Delete all data removes everything Till Morning stored and turns off
  all shields. Deleting the app does the same.
- **With us:** the data we hold is anonymous, so we usually can't link it to you. If you want us to
  try, email [support email] with the date you subscribed and we'll help.

## Children

Till Morning is not made for children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect their data.

## Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete personal data, or object to how we use it, by emailing
[support email]. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and, for big changes, tell you in the app.
