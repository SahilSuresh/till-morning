## Common questions

**A shield didn't appear when I opened a shopping app.**
Check Settings → Screen Time on your iPhone and make sure Till Morning still has access. Then open
Till Morning → Apps and check the app is shielded. Shields can take a moment to appear after a restart.

**My Shopping Pass ended but the app didn't lock straight away.**
iPhone sometimes delivers Screen Time timers a little late. The app locks again within a minute or two.

**How do I cancel?**
On your iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Till Morning → Cancel. If you cancel, all
shields are removed when your plan ends. Your XP, rank and settings are kept.

**I changed phones. How do I get my subscription back?**
Open Till Morning → Settings → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.
Your XP and history stay on the old phone, because there's no account.

**How do I turn Till Morning off?**
Settings → Shield → turn shields off, or delete the app. Removing Screen Time access also turns off all shields.

**How do I delete my data?**
Till Morning → Settings → Delete all data. See the [privacy policy]([privacy URL]) for the anonymous data we hold.
