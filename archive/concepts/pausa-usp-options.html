The finding that changes the pitch

The "no-buy" and "wait before you buy" shelf on the App Store is busier than I said in round 1. UnBuy, NoBuy, Stop Impulse Buying, SpendWise and Buy Later all offer wait lists, streaks or no-buy challenges, several with no account and on-device data, like Pausa.

From their listings, they all run on willpower: you log things, and nothing stops you opening the shop. Pausa is the only one with a real lock. So the pitch could be "the no-buy app with teeth": blockers have teeth but don't care about money, and no-buy apps care about money but have no teeth. (The "no lock" part comes from reading their store listings, not from installing each app.)

Pausa with Park it

Blockers sell time. Opal, one sec and Brick ration minutes or add friction to opening an app. They don't care what you were about to buy.

Wait-list apps sell patience. SpendWise and Buy Later let you share a product into a 30-day list, but nothing stops you buying it anyway.

Nobody joins the two. A shield that says "park it instead", plus a list that brings the item back when you're calm, is the gap. Neither half is new on its own; together they are.

Why it's unique

Blockers fight the app. Pausa takes the want seriously, keeps it safe and hands it back when you're calm. "Not never, just not tonight" fits the no-shaming tone.

Why iPhone allows it

A Share extension only sees what the person chooses to share (a link, title and picture). It is on-device, needs no account, and the night schedule uses the same Screen Time tools Pausa already plans.

What it fixes

App Review 4.3: a share extension, a wait list and a morning review are clearly not Onus or Opal. Retention: there's a reason to open Pausa every morning. And it can launch under Finance, not Productivity.

One rule this tempts us to bend

Parked items often carry a price, so it's tempting to show "£212 you didn't spend" on the let-go shelf. That is the most powerful number we could show, but it breaks the "no money-saved figures" rule, because Pausa can't know you didn't buy it elsewhere. My take: keep the rule and show the count plus pictures. If you want to test the number, label it "Price of things you let go", never "saved".

Five smaller ideas that stack on top

None of these is a USP on its own, but each makes Pausa feel more like a spending coach and less like a lock. They're ranked by how much they help, for how little build.

What I'd do

  1. Make "Park it till morning" the headline. New App Store line: "Don't stop shopping. Sleep on it."
  2. Add it to the pause. "Remind me in 1 hour" becomes "Park it till morning", and after 23:00 the pause only offers Leave it or Park it.
  3. Add risky hours and the voice note as small extras that make the pause feel personal.
  4. Save sale season for the first Black Friday after launch, as a marketing push.
  5. Leave the buddy for version 2.
