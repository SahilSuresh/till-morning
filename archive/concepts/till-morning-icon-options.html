Chosen: Sleepy bag

The basket goes to sleep till morning

A shopping bag with its eyes closed, on the app's electric blue. It says what the app is about (shopping) and what it does (lets the want sleep) in one glance, with no words.

  • Instantly reads as "shopping", which a pause or moon icon can't do.
  • Stands apart from the many pause-symbol apps, which also helps with Apple's "too similar" rule (4.3).
  • Friendly like the app's sticker mascot, and calm, not shouty.
  • Still clear at 29 px in Settings and as a round TikTok profile picture.
