Pausa, combined: every want waits its turn

Version 2 · redesigned with ScreensDesign references

All the ideas from rounds 1 and 2 now live in one app. The heart of it is the waiting room. Anything you want goes in as a paper tag. How long it waits depends on the price, the hour and the season. Planned treats skip the queue, and letting something go earns a parcel. Try it on the phone, or jump to any idea from the list on the right.

23:10●●● ▮

The three laws of the waiting room

1

Unplanned wants wait. One hour for every £10 once it's over £20. Anything that would be ready at night waits until 08:00. In a sale week, waits are doubled.

2

Planned treats skip the queue. Friday takeaway that you planned on Sunday opens with a hello, not a pause.

3

Letting go earns a parcel. Walk away, or say "let it go" in the morning, and you get a sticker parcel to open. You get the unboxing without the spending.
