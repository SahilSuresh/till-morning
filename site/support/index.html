Setting up

Open Till Morning and follow the welcome screens. When asked, allow Screen Time access and pick the shopping and takeaway apps you want a pause in front of. Till Morning never learns which apps you picked: Apple keeps that private.

The pause doesn't appear

  • Check your plan is active: Settings → your plan. The shields need Till Morning Pro (the first 7 nights are free).
  • Check the shields are on: the Shields card on Today says "Shields on".
  • Check Screen Time access: iPhone Settings → Screen Time → Apps with Screen Time Access → Till Morning is on.
  • Pick your apps again: Today → Shields card → Your apps.

Shopping Pass

On Today, tap Get a pass, say what you need and set a limit and a timer. The app opens for those minutes and locks again after.

Night lock

Overnight your shop apps stay closed. Change the hours, or turn it off, in Settings → Night lock.

Subscription

  • Cancel any time in your iPhone Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the free week or period ends to avoid the next charge.
  • New phone? Tap Settings → Restore purchases in Till Morning.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Deleting your data

Everything Till Morning keeps is on your phone. Settings → Delete all data clears it, and deleting the app removes it completely. See the privacy policy.
