Make the name the feature: lock the night, trust the lock, feed the takeaway craving

I checked 10 rivals and what people trying to stop late-night buying actually say. Nobody pairs a real app lock with a "wait till morning" rule. That pairing is ours, so the next update should make it louder rather than add more parts.

Top 3 for the next update
1

Night lock

From 23:00 to 07:00 the shield offers only "Park it till morning" or "Close". No Shopping Pass at night, with a slow emergency door. It turns the app's name into a rule people can feel.

NowEffort SHard to copy: the name
2

Backup dinner

People write two or three easy meals they already have at home. On takeaway apps, the pause shows them: "You've got freezer pizza or eggs on toast." No rival helps with food at all.

NowEffort SNobody does it
3

Shields check and safe off

A quiet "Shields working, checked 2 min ago" line, a warning if Screen Time access drops, and a clear "Turn off till tomorrow". This answers the biggest rival complaint: blocks that break or trap people.

Audience

Who we're for and what hurts

UK adults, mostly 18 to 34, who buy on their phone when tired, alone or bored, often in bed after 22:00. They want to shop on purpose, not quit shopping. They delete apps that nag, shame, break, or lock them out of their own phone.

Double down on what only we have

Our edges, made louder

Lock plus wait

Make "Park it till morning" the first thing in App Store screenshot 1 and the opening of every TikTok: open Deliveroo at 23:40, sleepy bag appears, parked, morning card at 08:00 asks "Still want it?". Night lock (below) makes this a rule, not a tip.

The torn ticket

The tear-to-start Shopping Pass is the most filmable moment in the app. Add a soft paper-tear sound (off with Quiet mode) and use a slow-motion tear as screenshot 2.

Honest, not guessy

Rivals show "money saved" they can't know. Put our line on screenshot 4: "No bank links. No made-up savings. Just the nights you slept on it." Show the privacy label "Data Not Collected" too.

Made for the UK

Name Deliveroo, Just Eat, Klarna and Clearpay in the listing subtitle and keywords. No rival does. Launch in January with the no-buy month.

Lock Screen widget

"2 parked till morning" plus the night lock moon. Visible every time they reach for the phone, great in screenshots. Effort M (WidgetKit, App Group). Opal and CartPause have one; we don't. No review risk.

Friction tune-up

A 5-tick checklist per app: turn off 1-Click, remove saved card, sign out of SHEIN, unsubscribe from sale emails. Matches what people already do ("friction maxxing"). No XP, since we can't check it. Effort S, deep links only.

Quiet mode, free

One switch for haptics and sounds, never behind the paywall. one sec users are leaving over paid haptics. Effort S.

Later

When 1.0 has users

Your risky hour, learned

From shield taps logged on the phone, suggest "Thursdays around 23:00 are your hardest" and offer to start night lock earlier. Pass For Now sells this. Effort M; needs a few weeks of data.

Payday and sale-week lock

Pick your payday; the shield is a little stronger for 2 days after, like the sale-week shield. Effort S. Assumed pain, needs testing.
