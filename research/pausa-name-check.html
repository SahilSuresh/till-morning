The two paths

A. Keep Pausa, longer title

Not recommended
Pausa: Impulse Buy Pause
  • Apple allows it. Names only have to be unique as a whole and 30 characters or fewer, so this 24-character title would pass.
  • But the UK App Store already has 9 apps called "Pausa: …", from menopause trackers to breathing apps.
  • One of them is the same idea. "Pausa: Screen Time & Focus" adds a calm pause before apps you pick, uses Apple's Screen Time, needs no account and keeps data on the phone. People who hear "Pausa" will find that app first.
  • Trademark: the EU mark PAUSA belongs to a cheese-snack maker (food only), so the legal risk is low. But with this many Pausas around, you could not protect the name yourself.

B. New name

Recommended
Till Morning: No Impulse Buys
  • You own the search. Anyone who hears the name on TikTok can find the app straight away.
  • It tells the story. It matches the hero feature "Park it till morning" and the late-night launch pitch for No-buy January.
  • You can protect it. A clean name is worth registering as a UK trade mark before launch.
  • Cost: rename the prototype and the docs. Nothing is on the App Store yet, so this is the cheapest moment to switch.
