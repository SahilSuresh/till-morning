Final recommendation

One winner

WINNER

Till Morning

App Store: Till Morning: No Impulse Buys · subtitle Pause shopping & takeaway apps

  1. The name is the promise. Tonight's want waits for the 08:00 "still want it?" check. It matches the hero feature and the line "Your 11 PM basket can wait till morning".
  2. It covers both jobs. Shopping and takeaway are both late-night urges; Anti-Haul and Calm Cart only cover shopping.
  3. Clean store namespace. No app called Till Morning on the App Store (UK, US) or Google Play.
  4. It owns an empty spot. No rival uses time-of-day naming.
  5. It is the punchline in a creator video. "It's 1:12am, your basket says £86.40… it can wait Till Morning."
  6. Usable domains are free (tillmorning.co.uk, tillmorningapp.com), and the .com is buyable later from a broker if the app takes off.

What it costs you: the exact handles @tillmorning are taken on X and TikTok (use @tillmorningapp). A US kids' banking app is called "Till" and holds a class 9 TILL mark, so get a lawyer's view before a US marketing push. Some people will write "Til Morning", so claim that spelling too when you claim handles (free).

What would change this: if a UK or EU search finds a TILL MORNING mark in software classes, switch to Backup #1.
