Blockers can’t submit without these

2.5.1entitlementYou + builder

Family Controls approval not requested

Shields can’t ship to the App Store without Apple’s distribution approval, and it can take weeks.

Fix: request it now for the app and for every Screen Time extension (shield look, shield buttons, activity monitor), each by its own bundle ID.

5.1.1(i)1.53.1.2(c)You

No privacy, support or terms pages

Apple needs a privacy policy and a support page with real contact details, plus a terms link for subscriptions. The prototype’s Terms and Privacy links only show a toast.

Fix: one free page set (GitHub Pages or a public Notion page, no domain needed). For terms, link Apple’s standard EULA. Link all three in App Store Connect and on the paywall and in Settings.

2.3.74.1(c)5.2.1You

“Pausa” is taken in spirit

“Pausa: Screen Time & Focus” already sells the same calm pause-before-apps idea, and 9 UK apps start with Pausa. Expect a copycat or confusion flag.

Fix: pick Till Morning or Kip On It and reserve it by creating the App Store Connect record.

High risk fix before submitting

2.1(a)Prototype + notes

The reviewer may never see a shield

Apple’s review iPhone probably has no Amazon, SHEIN or Deliveroo, so “Shopping & Food” in the picker can be empty and the core feature looks broken.

Fix: add “No shopping apps? Pick any app to try it” on the picker step, and tell the reviewer in the notes to shield Books or Podcasts.

2.5.1Builder

Shield button opening Pausa directly

“I still want to shop” jumping straight into the pause relies on iOS 26.5. I couldn’t find a documented public way for a shield button to open its app; developer forum threads still ask for one.

Fix: use the notification route on every iOS version unless Apple documents an API. No private tricks, because those get rejected.

3.1.25.6Prototype

“Remind me on day 5” with no notification permission

The paywall promises a reminder, but setup never asks for notifications. A trial reminder that silently never arrives looks like a trap.

Fix: add the notifications step from the ship plan. If someone declines, the toggle says “Turn on notifications to get this reminder.” Pausa must still work without them.

2.5.192.3.1(a)Prototype + notes

Features that unlock over week one

The reviewer only sees day 1. Apple treats features it can’t find as hidden or undocumented.

Fix: list every feature in the review notes, and add the “Everything Pausa can do” list in Settings with a “Show all now” switch. Remove the test panel and every “(simulated)” label from the real app.

5.6.1Builder

Rating pop-up must be Apple’s own

The prototype draws its own star sheet after “Let it go”. Apple doesn’t allow custom review prompts.

Fix: keep the same timing rules but call Apple’s review request. The Settings “Rate Pausa” row opens the App Store review page.

Draft review notes

Paste into App Store Connect, App Review Information, Notes. Swap in the final name.

Pausa adds a short pause before the shopping and takeaway apps a person picks, so they shop on purpose instead of on impulse. It uses the Screen Time API (FamilyControls, ManagedSettings, DeviceActivity). Everything stays on the device: no account, no login, no server, no bank connection.

HOW TO TEST
1. Complete the short setup and allow Screen Time when asked.
2. In Apple's app picker, choose any installed app. A review device may have no shopping apps, so Books or Podcasts works fine.
3. Start the free trial on the paywall (sandbox). The yearly and monthly subscriptions are on that screen; Restore purchases is below them and in Settings.
4. Open the app you picked. Pausa's shield appears. Tap "I still want to shop" to get the 15-second pause, then choose "I'll leave it", "Remind me in 1 hour" or "Shop with a plan".
5. "Shop with a plan" starts a timed Shopping Pass: the app opens for the chosen minutes, then locks again automatically and Pausa asks a short check-in.

FEATURES THAT APPEAR DURING THE FIRST WEEK
Some features are introduced one a day so new users aren't overwhelmed. To see them all at once: Settings > Everything Pausa can do > Show all now. They are: [list the week-one features].

WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT FROM SCREEN TIME BLOCKERS
Pausa is about spending, not screen time. It never fully blocks an app: it slows down impulse buys, offers a timed Shopping Pass with a spending limit, and lets people park a want until morning.

OTHER NOTES
- Notifications are optional. They are used for the trial reminder and the pass check-in.
- The optional Safari extension only reads shop checkout pages, on the device.
- Checkout screenshots are picked by the user with the system photo picker and read on the device.
- When the trial or subscription ends, all shields are removed.

Can’t verify from here

  • App Privacy label. “Data Not Collected” is only true if the paywall has no tracking SDK. A paywall SDK such as RevenueCat usually means declaring purchases and an identifier.
  • £29.88 as a price. Apple only offers fixed price points. If £29.88 isn’t one, use £29.99 and show “£2.50 a month”.
  • EU storefronts. Selling in the EU needs a trader declaration that shows your contact details publicly. A UK-only launch avoids that for now.
  • Shield opening the app on iOS 26.5, and the pass re-lock timing. Both need a real iPhone.
  • Age rating. Expected 4+. Answer Apple’s questionnaire honestly.
  • Crashes. There’s no TestFlight build yet, so no crash data to check.
